BEFORE YOU BOOK

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS.

How much does a shoot cost?

Quotes depend on the type of shoot, duration, location and deliverables. Include your budget and what you need in the inquiry so we can discuss the right scope.

Where do you shoot?

8B Production is based in Atlanta. Include your preferred location or venue when you inquire. For projects outside metro Atlanta, include travel details so those costs can be discussed.

Can I bring a creative concept?

Yes. Share your inspiration, outfit ideas and intended use for the photographs. We can discuss locations and what the concept needs.

How do I book?

Use the booking form or email booking@8bproduction.com. Availability and booking details must be confirmed directly.
